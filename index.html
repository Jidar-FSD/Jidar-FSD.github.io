TubeForge

A private desktop tool that produces short science and nature videos from real, openly licensed footage and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

Uses YouTube API Services. TubeForge uploads videos to, and reads statistics of, its owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. By using TubeForge you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings.
Earth Unrendered Real footage of Earth's natural phenomena and the science behind them.
Ocean Unrendered Real underwater footage of ocean animals, from coral reefs to the deep sea.
Wild Unrendered Real footage of wild animals and the most surprising true facts about them.

What it does

For each channel, TubeForge picks a subject, finds public-domain and openly licensed footage of it, checks that the footage really shows the subject, writes and fact-checks a short narration, and edits a vertical video with an AI-generated voice. Footage whose licence asks for credit (CC BY) is credited in the video's description, and each channel states that its narration is AI-generated.

Who uses it

TubeForge runs on its owner's computer and is used only to manage the owner's own channels. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
