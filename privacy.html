Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

TubeForge ("the app") is a private desktop tool that its owner uses to publish videos to, and read statistics of, the owner's own YouTube channels (Earth Unrendered, Ocean Unrendered and Wild Unrendered). This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • With the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl permissions: the channel's identity (name and channel ID), and the ability to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publishing schedule, and upload thumbnails for videos the app itself produced.
  • With the yt-analytics.readonly permission: aggregate statistics of the channel's own videos, such as views, watch time, average view percentage, likes, comments and subscribers gained.

The app does not access other users' channels, private messages, comments' authors, payment information or any personal data of viewers.

How the data is used and stored

  • Upload permissions are used only to publish videos the owner's copy of the app produced, to the channel the owner signed in with.
  • Statistics are used only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform and to choose future topics.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • Stored statistics are refreshed at least every 30 days and deleted when the owner removes the app's data or revokes access.
  • Deleting data: revoking access (below) stops all further access at once; the app then deletes its stored sign-in token and statistics for that channel. A deletion request can also be sent to the contact address below and is completed within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After access is revoked the app can no longer upload or read statistics, and its stored sign-in token stops working.

Cookies

The app is a desktop program and does not place cookies in any browser. This website sets no cookies.
